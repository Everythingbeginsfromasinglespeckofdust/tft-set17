**Final Reality Audit Verdict**: **`RUNTIME_MISMATCH_FOUND`**

## 1. Executive Truth Statement
A rigorous, line-by-line runtime execution trace was conducted comparing the actual code execution against the user's visual observations.

The user's observations are **100% FACTUAL and VALID**:
1. **Empty Hex Bounding Boxes**: Pre-rendered video clip (`overlay_verification_eda87ad9_300s_360s.mp4`) contained pre-baked false-positive bounding boxes generated by `board_recognizer.py` with low threshold ($0.52$).
2. **Shop Recognition Discrepancy**: `ShopRecognizerV2` had `min_confidence_threshold = 0.10`, causing noisy template matches ($0.15 \sim 0.25$) to be labeled `RECOGNIZED` instead of `UNKNOWN`/`LOW_CONFIDENCE`.
3. **Hardcoded Fallback in Decision Pipeline**: `src/tft/decision/analysis_manager.py` had hardcoded `board_units=[Unit(champion="Miss Fortune", cost=3, star_level=2)]` and `bench_units=[Unit(champion="Miss Fortune", cost=3, star_level=1)]` as placeholders.
